HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

The floor is a little slippery: you get up to speed fast but glide a short way when you let go. Walls of blocks sweep toward you in waves of eight rows. Get past all eight and WAVE SURVIVED goes up by one.

On the main menu, pick your outfit under SKINS, paint your own blocks under BLOCKS, and build your own waves under WAVES: fly over the arena and stack the blocks yourself.

Slip through the holes, jump the low parts, dive, or jump on top and ride. A wall that catches you pushes you toward the edge. Fall off the front or back edge, or touch a laser fence on the sides, and the run is over.

Rival runners (set how many in Settings) dodge the same walls and pick fights. Punch them off the arena or into a laser before they do it to you. Punch in the middle of a dive for a Super Punch: you keep all your speed and hit much harder.

OBSTACLES

Stacked blocksTwo or three cubes high, too tall to jump. If a wall catches you it pushes you back, so slide sideways into a hole before you run out of floor.
Single blockOne cube. A jump gets you on top, and a running jump or a dive clears it. Ride it, hop off, or climb from it onto a stack.
Climb wallThree blocks high with no hole (Normal and harder). Its lighter lane has steps in front: jump onto the low step, then the middle one, then over the top, and drop down behind it.
CrusherRises and slams down. Get caught underneath and you're bonked away.
SpikesA block covered in spikes, one or two high. You can't stand on it, and touching it from any side knocks you backwards with a tumble.
Breakable blockCracked glass, solid like any block. Dive into it (or belly-slide into it) and it shatters, however little of it you hit. One dive smashes one block of a stack: break the bottom one and the one above stays where it is.
BarA low striped bar lying across two squares. Hop over it: touch it and you're knocked backwards.
Laser fencesThey stand right beside the outer lanes, so there is no way around a wall at the sides. Touch one and the run is over.

DIFFICULTY

Pick a difficulty on the main menu, from Very easy to Very hard. The easy levels have slower walls, more time to react and no climbing. Normal mixes in the odd climb wall, and Hard and Very hard are faster, with lots of parkour. Your personal best is kept separately for each difficulty.

Within a run the blocks get 5% faster every few waves and harder patterns unlock. Each wave has its own colour, a new wall comes at most every 7 seconds, and the next wave only starts once the last one has left the map. Every wave is checked by the pattern engine, so there is always a way through: a hole on the ground, or the steps of a climb wall.

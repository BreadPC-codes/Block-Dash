HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

Walls of blocks sweep toward you in waves of eight rows. Get past all eight and WAVE SURVIVED goes up by one.

Slip through the holes, jump the low parts, dive, or jump on top and ride. A wall that catches you pushes you toward the edge. Get shoved off or touch a live laser and the run is over.

CONTROLS

OBSTACLES

Stacked blocksTwo or three blocks high, too tall to jump. If a wall catches you it pushes you back, so slide sideways into a hole before you run out of floor.
Single blockAs tall as you. One jump gets you on top. Ride it, hop off, or climb from it onto a stack.
CrusherRises and slams down. Get caught underneath and you're bonked away.
Laser fenceLines both edges and switches on and off. Only the skilled get around the blocks that way.

DIFFICULTY

The blocks start slow and get 5% faster every 20 waves. Each wave has its own colour. Harder patterns unlock as you go, the lasers stay on a bit longer, and from wave 6 a pattern can arrive mirrored. Every wave is checked by the pattern engine, so there is always a way through on the ground.
